## E001

**Error:** Envelope {name} is used but was never declared.

Declare it with `custom "envelope" "open" "<name>"` on or after `start`, or use the reserved value `pool` to send the posting to the pool instead. Reported once for each posting that resolves to the name, so a sale booked across several tax lots repeats it.


## E002

**Error:** Account {account} has no envelope metadata and posts in a cash-moving transaction.

Add `envelope: "<name>"` (or `envelope: "pool"`) to the account's `open`, to the transaction, or to the posting.


## E003

**Error:** Envelope {name} is declared twice, or used outside its open-to-close dates.

Declare each envelope exactly once, and only use it between its `open` and its `close`. Remove the duplicate `open`, move the postings and allocations inside that window, or declare the envelope on or before the date you first use it.


## E004

**Error:** `cash-account` is set on {account}, which is not valid there.

`cash-account` marks spendable cash and may only be boolean `TRUE` on an Assets or Liabilities account. `FALSE` is accepted anywhere and means the same as omitting the key. Remove a non-boolean value, or set it to `TRUE` on a valid account.


## E005

**Error:** Envelope {name} is closed with a non-zero balance of {balance}.

Allocate the difference back to the pool, or allocate it out to another envelope, before closing.


## E006

**Error:** Currency {currency} on {date} is not the operating currency {operating_currency}.

Only the operating currency is supported. A commodity posting with a cost or price in the operating currency is accepted, so securities bought with cash are fine. Re-denominate the posting, or set the correct `operating_currency` option.


## E007

**Error:** Invariant fails for {month}: pool plus envelopes is {actual} but cash is {expected} (difference {difference}).

At each month end the pool plus all envelope balances must equal the ledger's cash balance. A mismatch means a posting was missed or unsupported; check for unenveloped accounts or other currencies.


## E008

**Error:** Invalid envelope metadata: {detail}.

Check the types: `monthly` must be a number or "avgN", `up-to` a positive number, `priority` a number, `goal` an amount, `by` a date and `parent` a string.


## E009

**Error:** The ledger does not declare a single operating currency.

Set `option "operating_currency" "USD"` (or your own currency). Envelope budgeting supports one currency only.


## E010

**Error:** Envelope {name} has a plan that has no effect: {detail}.

A plan funds an envelope either by `monthly` (with an optional `up-to` ceiling) or by a savings `goal` with a `by` date, never both. `goal` without `by`, `by` without `goal`, `monthly` with `goal`, and `up-to` with `goal` are all errors because the ignored key would otherwise have no effect. A goal is funded by cumulative contributions, so once the total is saved it stays met even after the money is spent. Remove the unused or conflicting key, or start a fresh plan with `unplan`.


## W001

**Warning:** The pool is negative on {date}, reaching a low of {balance} (it opened that day at {opening}).

More was allocated than the cash available at that point. Fund the envelope after the income arrives, or move money back from another envelope. Run `beancount-envelope-budget pool LEDGER` to see the pool's per-transaction ledger around the dip, with a running balance.


## W002

**Warning:** {kind} for {name} is dated before the start directive and is ignored.

Allocations and `open` directives dated before the `start` directive are ignored. Move them to on or after the start date.


## W003

**Warning:** There is no start directive, so envelope budgeting does nothing.

Add `custom "envelope" "start"` dated when you want tracking to begin.
